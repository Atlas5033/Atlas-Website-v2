What care can include

Your first appointment

After your phone consultation, your first clinical appointment is by video. We’ll review your health history, current concerns, and goals. You’ll have time to ask questions and understand the recommended next steps.

Buprenorphine treatment

We help people start or continue buprenorphine treatment, including Suboxone, when clinically appropriate.

  • Home induction when clinically appropriate
  • Ongoing monitoring and dose adjustment
  • Overdose prevention and harm reduction

Ongoing support

Follow-up visits review your medication response, cravings, side effects, and recovery goals, with timing based on your individual needs. Care includes medication management, recovery support, and attention to mental health concerns affecting your recovery.

  • Depression and anxiety
  • Trauma-related symptoms
  • Co-occurring psychiatric concerns
  • Referral for specialized care when needed

Coordinating your care

With your permission, we can work with your primary care clinician, therapist, or other providers already involved in your care.

  • Lab and monitoring coordination
  • Connections to additional services when needed

Insurance and payment

We accept select Colorado insurance plans and offer self-pay appointments. Coverage and out-of-pocket costs vary by plan.

At-home testing is included in your care. In-person lab testing may sometimes be needed.
