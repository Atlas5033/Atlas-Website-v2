Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Atlas Recovery, PLLC respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website.

Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information when requesting an appointment, submitting a professional referral, completing a contact form, or communicating with Atlas Recovery.

Appointment requests, contact forms, referrals, and patient portal functions may be provided through PracticeQ or IntakeQ, which are third-party services used by Atlas Recovery.

Website Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to help us understand general website traffic and usage patterns. Google Analytics may collect information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate geographic location, and interactions with the website.

Atlas Recovery does not use website analytics to sell personal information or for targeted advertising.

Protected Health Information

This general website is not intended for the transmission of urgent, sensitive, or detailed medical information except through forms or systems specifically identified as secure.

Information submitted through secure clinical systems may be protected by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), 42 CFR Part 2 when applicable, and other privacy laws.

Atlas Recovery's Notice of Privacy Practices provides additional information about how protected health information may be used and disclosed and how patients may exercise their privacy rights.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, review referrals, coordinate appointment requests, provide requested services, operate and improve the website, maintain security, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Third-Party Links and Services

This website may link to third-party services, including PracticeQ, IntakeQ, patient portal services, insurance websites, and other resources. Those services maintain their own privacy and security practices.

Data Security

Atlas Recovery uses reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards designed to protect information. No website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for individuals seeking information about Atlas Recovery's services. We do not knowingly use this website to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization.

Changes to This Policy

Atlas Recovery may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the Atlas Recovery contact form.
