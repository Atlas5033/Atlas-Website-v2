Our Philosophy

Healthcare should stay human.

Good treatment is more than making the right clinical decision. It means listening, explaining what comes next, and following through—especially when things get complicated.

Atlas is a small, clinician-owned practice. Your care isn't passed through a large treatment platform—you'll know who is responsible for your treatment.

Meet the Founder

Aaron Jones, MSN, PMHNP-BC

I'm a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with a clinical focus on addiction treatment and recovery-focused psychiatric care. My approach is grounded in gentle curiosity: listening without judgment, asking thoughtful questions, and creating space for you to feel comfortable being honest about what is going on in your life. From there, we'll talk through your options clearly and build treatment around your goals.

Before entering healthcare, I served as a Peace Corps Volunteer and later in U.S. Army Special Forces. Those experiences shaped my belief in practical support, personal dignity, and following through when people need help.

Early in my healthcare career, I was struck by how easily people struggling with addiction could be stigmatized within the healthcare system. A substance use disorder could change the way someone was perceived and treated, often at a time when they were most vulnerable. My work in psychiatry and addiction care has reinforced something I continue to believe: good treatment requires both sound medicine and respect for the person receiving it.

I founded Atlas Recovery to put those principles into practice. I wanted to create addiction treatment that is evidence-based and clinically responsible without being unnecessarily difficult, judgmental, or impersonal.

Atlas is intentionally small and clinician-owned. As we grow, my goal is to build these principles into the practice itself, so thoughtful, respectful care doesn't depend on any one clinician—it's simply how Atlas provides care.

For Professionals

Trusted Partners in Recovery

Atlas partners with primary care practices, therapists, hospitals, treatment programs, and recovery organizations to provide timely, evidence-based outpatient treatment for opioid use disorder through telehealth across Colorado.

We make referrals straightforward, contact patients promptly, and—with appropriate authorization—coordinate with the professionals involved in their care.

Appropriate Referrals

  • Evaluation for opioid use disorder or buprenorphine treatment
  • Initiation or continuation of outpatient buprenorphine treatment
  • Transition from detoxification, residential treatment, hospitalization, or another prescriber
  • Co-occurring psychiatric concerns that can be safely managed in an outpatient setting
  • Adults located in Colorado who can participate safely in telehealth care

Services

  • Opioid use disorder evaluation and treatment planning
  • Buprenorphine initiation, continuation, and monitoring
  • Recovery-focused psychiatric medication management when appropriate
  • Overdose prevention and harm-reduction support
  • Care coordination with referring providers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Starting treatment can raise a lot of questions. Here are answers to some of the ones we hear most often.

Do you prescribe Suboxone® (buprenorphine)?

Yes. Medication for opioid use disorder is available following an individualized clinical evaluation.

Do I need a referral?

No. Most patients can schedule directly without a referral.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. Atlas Recovery is currently in-network with Anthem, Aetna, and eligible Cigna Healthcare behavioral health plans administered through Evernorth. Additional Colorado plans are being added.

How soon can I be seen?

Appointment availability varies, but we strive to schedule new patients as quickly as possible.

Are appointments virtual?

Yes. All appointments are provided through secure telehealth for patients located in Colorado.

What if I'm not sure treatment is right for me?

That's perfectly okay. Schedule a free consultation and we can discuss your concerns, answer questions, and determine whether Atlas Recovery is the right fit.

Can you treat patients anywhere in Colorado?

Yes. Atlas Recovery provides secure telehealth services to patients located throughout Colorado. You must be physically located in Colorado at the time of your appointment.

Will my information remain confidential?

Yes. Your care is protected by HIPAA and other applicable privacy laws. Information is shared only with your permission or when disclosure is required or permitted by law.
