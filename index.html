Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Starting treatment can raise a lot of questions. Here are answers to some of the ones we hear most often.

How do I start if I’m not sure treatment is right for me?

Start with a free 15-minute phone consultation. No referral is needed. We’ll discuss your concerns and see whether Atlas is a good fit before scheduling your first clinical appointment.

Do you prescribe Suboxone® (buprenorphine)?

Yes. Medication for opioid use disorder is available following an individualized clinical evaluation.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept select Colorado plans, with coverage varying by plan and network. View accepted insurance plans and self-pay pricing.

How soon will I hear from you?

We aim to contact you within one business day to discuss next steps and appointment availability.

Are visits online, and where can I receive care?

Your consultation is by phone and clinical visits are by secure video. You must be physically in Colorado for your visits.

Will my information remain confidential?

Yes. Your care is protected by HIPAA and other applicable privacy laws. Information is shared only with your permission or when disclosure is required or permitted by law.
